### Knowledge Gaps Identified

**Identity Theory**
- Mind-brain identity theory (Smart, Place)
- Mental states ARE brain states (not just correlated, but identical)
- "Pain = C-fibre firing" type claims

**Kripke's Objection**
- Saul Kripke's argument against identity theory
- Uses rigid designators and necessity
- If pain IS C-fibres firing, should be necessarily true (like water IS H2O)
- But seems contingent - this is the objection
